Your Mountain Escape

Discover the ultimate Smoky Mountain log cabin retreat where rustic charm meets modern luxury.

Just 2 miles from vibrant Downtown Gatlinburg and 1 mile from the thrilling Ober Mountain, Classy Bear Cabin offers breathtaking mountain views that will leave you speechless.

Perfect for families or groups seeking both adventure and relaxation, our fully renovated cabin comfortably sleeps 9 guests with premium amenities at every turn.
