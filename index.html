Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Classy Bear Cabin

How do I save money by booking direct?

When you book directly at classybearcabin.com, you save 15% off the nightly rate plus avoid OTA service fees (which can be $100-150+). On a typical 2-night stay, you'll save around $280. Plus you get direct owner communication and more flexible policies.

How far is the cabin from downtown Gatlinburg?

Classy Bear Cabin is just 2 miles from downtown Gatlinburg (5 minutes by car), 5 minutes from Ober Mountain ski resort, and 6 minutes from Ripley's Aquarium. We're in Chalet Village at summit elevation with stunning views.

Is the cabin pet-friendly?

Yes! Classy Bear Cabin welcomes well-behaved pets. Contact us for our pet policy details and any applicable pet fees. We want your whole family to enjoy the Smokies!

What's included in the kitchen?

Our newly renovated kitchen (2026) is fully equipped with stainless steel appliances, cookware, dishes, utensils, coffee maker, toaster, and microwave. We also provide starter supplies: coffee, dish soap, paper towels, and trash bags.

What's the cancellation policy?

Direct bookings have flexible cancellation. Full refund if cancelled 14+ days before check-in. 50% refund if cancelled 7-14 days before. Contact us for special circumstances — we're reasonable and want to work with you.

Is 4WD required in winter?

The cabin is located at a summit elevation in Chalet Village. In winter weather (snow/ice), 4WD or AWD with good tires is strongly recommended. The roads are maintained but can be steep and winding.
