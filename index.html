Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Classy Bear Cabin

How many guests can Classy Bear Cabin accommodate?

Classy Bear Cabin sleeps up to 9 guests comfortably - 6 adults and 3 children. The cabin features 3 private suites with king beds and a loft with bunk beds and trundle for kids.

Does Classy Bear Cabin have a hot tub?

Yes! Classy Bear Cabin features a private hot tub on the screened patio with stunning mountain views - perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring the Smokies.

How far is the cabin from downtown Gatlinburg?

Classy Bear Cabin is just 2 miles from downtown Gatlinburg and 1 mile from Ober Mountain ski resort. You're close to all the action while enjoying peaceful mountain seclusion.

What amenities are included?

Amenities include: hot tub, 3 king suites with private bathrooms, electric fireplaces, fully equipped gourmet kitchen, 4 smart TVs, high-speed WiFi, arcade games, Wii U, board games, and stunning mountain views.

Is it cheaper to book direct?

Yes! When you book direct through our website, you get the best available rate plus exclusive perks. Direct booking avoids third-party fees, and payments are secure through Stripe.

What is check-in and check-out time?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request for direct bookings.
