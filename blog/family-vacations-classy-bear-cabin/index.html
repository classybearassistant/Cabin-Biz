A Cabin Designed Around How Families Actually Travel

Families travel differently than couples or solo guests. You need room to spread out without losing each other entirely. You need spaces where the kids can be loud while the adults wind down quietly. You need a kitchen that can actually handle breakfast for nine people. Classy Bear was configured with all of that in mind.

The three king suites give parents and older guests their own private retreats, while the loft bunk room becomes a natural gathering point for younger travelers — a space that feels like theirs. The open-concept main floor connects the fully renovated kitchen to the living and dining areas, so the family stays in the same orbit even when everyone is doing something different.

At 3,000 feet in Chalet Village, the cabin offers summit views that change with the light and season. Mornings on the screened patio, coffee in hand, looking out over the ridgeline — that kind of unhurried start to the day is something families rarely get at home, and it becomes one of the quiet anchors of a stay here.
