Bringing Your Dog to a Pet-Friendly Cabin in Gatlinburg

Published November 1, 2026 • 4 min read

Most Gatlinburg cabins say no to dogs, and the ones that say yes often bury a long list of conditions. Classy Bear Cabin keeps it simple: up to two well-behaved dogs, a $125 pet fee per stay, and a few house rules. Here is what the trip looks like with your dog along.

How the cabin works for dogs

The cabin has a private entrance, two parking spaces right at the door, and a screened patio where a dog can lie in the shade while you are in the hot tub. The floors on the main level are hard-surface, which makes muddy paws a non-event. We ask that dogs stay off the beds and are never left unattended in the hot tub area, and that you bring your own crate if your dog is crated at home.

Starter supplies cover the humans; bring food, bowls and a towel for the dog. There is a hose outside for rinsing off trail mud.

Where dogs can go in the Smokies

Great Smoky Mountains National Park allows dogs on only two trails: the Gatlinburg Trail, which starts at the Sugarlands Visitor Center about 10 minutes from the cabin, and the Oconaluftee River Trail on the North Carolina side. Both are flat, paved or gravel, and follow a river. Dogs are also allowed in campgrounds, picnic areas and along roads, so scenic drives like Newfound Gap Road and the Cades Cove loop work with a dog in the car.

Outside the park, the Gatlinburg Parkway sidewalks are dog-friendly and busy with other dogs on weekends. Several downtown restaurants have patios that allow leashed dogs; ask before you sit. Ober Mountain's tramway does not take dogs, so plan that as a people-only afternoon.

The pet fee and how to book

The pet fee is $125 per stay, not per night, and covers up to two dogs. Add the number of pets when you book direct and the fee is applied automatically. Booking direct is 15% less than the same dates on Airbnb, and you deal with us rather than a platform if anything comes up.

Questions guests ask

How many dogs can I bring?

Up to two well-behaved dogs. The pet fee is $125 per stay.

Can dogs hike in Great Smoky Mountains National Park?

Only on the Gatlinburg Trail and the Oconaluftee River Trail, plus campgrounds, picnic areas and roads. Dogs are not allowed on other park trails.

Is there a fenced yard?

No. The cabin is on a wooded summit lot with a screened patio; dogs should be leashed outside.
