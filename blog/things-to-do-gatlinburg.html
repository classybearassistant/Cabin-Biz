Top 10 Things to Do Near Classy Bear Cabin

January 28, 2026 • 8 min read

Location, location, location. Classy Bear Cabin isn't just a beautiful retreat — it's perfectly positioned to explore everything the Smoky Mountains have to offer. Just 2 miles from downtown Gatlinburg and 1 mile from Ober Mountain, you're minutes from world-class attractions while enjoying peaceful mountain seclusion.

Here are our top picks for must-do experiences during your stay:

1. 🎿 Ober Mountain

1 mile from cabin

Take the aerial tramway from downtown for stunning views, then enjoy skiing, snowboarding, snow tubing (winter), or the alpine slide and wildlife encounter (year-round). The scenic chairlift offers incredible mountain vistas any season.

2. 🏞️ Great Smoky Mountains National Park

5 minutes to park entrance

America's most-visited national park is literally in your backyard. Hike to waterfalls, spot wildlife, drive scenic routes like Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail, or just pull over and soak in the views. Entry is free!

3. 🥃 Ole Smoky Moonshine

2 miles (downtown)

Tennessee's first legal moonshine distillery offers free tastings of their famous white lightning and flavored moonshines. Watch the distilling process and grab a jar to take home. The apple pie moonshine is legendary.

4. 🐠 Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies

2 miles (downtown)

One of the best aquariums in America, featuring a 340-foot underwater tunnel, touch tanks, and incredible marine life. Perfect for families and a great rainy-day activity.

5. 🍽️ The Peddler Steakhouse

2 miles

A Gatlinburg institution since 1976. Their aged steaks and famous salad bar are worth the visit. Pro tip: They deliver to cabins — perfect for a special dinner in!

6. 🛍️ The Village Shops

2 miles (downtown)

27 unique shops in a charming European-style village. From handmade crafts to gourmet foods to Christmas decorations year-round, it's the perfect place to find unique souvenirs.

7. 🎭 Dollywood

15 miles

Dolly Parton's famous theme park in nearby Pigeon Forge offers world-class rides, shows, and Southern hospitality. Their holiday festivals (especially Christmas) are spectacular.

8. 🌿 Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail

3 miles to entrance

This stunning 5.5-mile one-way loop road takes you through old-growth forest past historic cabins and rushing streams. Stop for short hikes to waterfalls like Grotto Falls (2.6 miles round trip).

9. 🍷 Sugarlands Distilling Company

3 miles

Award-winning craft spirits with a welcoming tasting room. Their Butter Pecan Sippin' Cream is dangerously delicious. Free tours available.

10. 🌄 Clingmans Dome

25 miles

The highest point in the Smokies (6,643 ft) offers 360-degree views from the observation tower. On clear days, you can see for 100 miles. Note: Road closed December-March.

Pro Tips from Your Hosts

  • Beat the crowds: Visit popular attractions early morning or late afternoon
  • Parking hack: Use the Gatlinburg trolley system ($2) to avoid downtown parking hassles
  • Best sunset spot: Right from your cabin deck, honestly!
  • Grocery run: There's a Publix in Pigeon Forge (15 min) for cabin meal supplies
