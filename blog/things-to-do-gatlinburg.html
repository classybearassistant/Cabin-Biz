Top 10 Things to Do Near Classy Bear Cabin

January 28, 2026 • 8 min read

Location, location, location. Classy Bear Cabin isn't just a beautiful retreat — it's perfectly positioned to explore everything the Smoky Mountains have to offer. Just 2 miles from downtown Gatlinburg and 1 mile from Ober Mountain, you're minutes from world-class attractions while enjoying peaceful mountain seclusion.

1. 🎿 Ober Mountain

1 mile from cabin

Take the aerial tramway for stunning views, then enjoy skiing, snowboarding, snow tubing (winter), or the alpine slide and wildlife encounter (year-round).

2. 🏞️ Great Smoky Mountains National Park

5 minutes to park entrance

America's most-visited national park is literally in your backyard. Hike to waterfalls, spot wildlife, or take scenic drives. Entry is free!

3. 🥃 Ole Smoky Moonshine

2 miles (downtown)

Tennessee's first legal moonshine distillery offers free tastings. The apple pie moonshine is legendary!

4. 🐠 Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies

2 miles (downtown)

One of the best aquariums in America, featuring a 340-foot underwater tunnel. Perfect for families!

5. 🍽️ The Peddler Steakhouse

2 miles

A Gatlinburg institution since 1976. Pro tip: They deliver to cabins!

6. 🛍️ The Village Shops

2 miles (downtown)

27 unique shops in a charming European-style village. Perfect for unique souvenirs.

7. 🎭 Dollywood

15 miles

Dolly Parton's famous theme park offers world-class rides, shows, and Southern hospitality.

8. 🌿 Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail

3 miles to entrance

Stunning 5.5-mile one-way loop through old-growth forest past historic cabins and rushing streams.

9. 🍷 Sugarlands Distilling Company

3 miles

Award-winning craft spirits with a welcoming tasting room. Try the Butter Pecan Sippin' Cream!

10. 🌄 Clingmans Dome

25 miles

The highest point in the Smokies (6,643 ft) offers 360-degree views. On clear days, see for 100 miles!
