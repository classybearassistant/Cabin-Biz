Meet NightFlight Expedition

Dollywood's brand-new NightFlight Expedition coaster opens in spring 2026 — a one-of-a-kind night-themed thrill ride combining glowing forest scenery, immersive sound, and unexpected drops. Expect long lines opening week. Plan your trip for late summer or early fall if you want to ride it without the wait.

It joins Dollywood's already legendary lineup of coasters: Lightning Rod (the world's fastest wood coaster), Big Bear Mountain, Wild Eagle, and Mystery Mine. There's a coaster for every age and bravery level.

Discounts & Deals

Save Money

Buy tickets online (not at the gate)

Online tickets typically save $5–10 per person, and they let you skip the ticket window line entirely. Buy at dollywood.com before you arrive.

2-day tickets are the best value

For most families, a 2-day ticket costs only slightly more than a 1-day ticket — and it transforms the experience. Day 1 is for the rides everyone HAS to do. Day 2 is for going back to favorites without rushing.

Season Pass math

If you're staying multiple days OR plan to visit again within a year, a Season Pass usually pays for itself in 2 visits. It also includes free parking and food/merchandise discounts.

Splash Country combo

If you're visiting June–August, the Splash Country water park is open and the combo ticket is a strong value, especially with kids. After a hot day on coasters, the lazy river is heaven.

Pro Tips to Beat the Crowds

Arrive Before Opening

Be at the gate 15–30 minutes before. The first 90 minutes have the shortest lines of the entire day.

Download the App First

Real-time wait times, show schedules, mobile food orders. Download before you arrive — Wi-Fi at the park is spotty.

Rainy Day? Head Inside

Indoor shows and exhibits empty out. The Heartsong Theater and Dolly Parton's Stampede are perfect rain backups.

Bring a Change of Clothes

Daredevil Falls and River Rampage will soak you. Lockers are available, but a change of clothes in the car is gold.

The Free Water Trick

Any food stand will give you a free cup of ice water on request. Saves $4 a bottle and beats summer heat.

Eat Early or Late

Lunch lines peak 12–1:30 PM. Eat at 11 or 2 and you'll save 30+ minutes of waiting.

Get the Cinnamon Bread

The Grist Mill cinnamon bread is iconic for a reason. Worth the line. Get there before 11 AM to skip it.

TimeSaver on Busy Days

The TimeSaver Pass adds up but transforms a Saturday in July. Worth it on summer weekends and during festivals.

Pack Like a Pro

Sunscreen, refillable water bottles, snacks (allowed!), portable phone charger, and comfortable shoes you've already broken in.

Best Rides for Every Age

NightFlight Expedition New · Spring 2026

Dollywood's newest coaster. Night-themed launch coaster with immersive scenery. Height 48".

Big Bear Mountain

Family-friendly launch coaster with mountain views and gentle thrills. Great first "big" coaster. Height 39".

Dragonflier

Suspended coaster designed for younger thrill-seekers. Smooth, soaring, perfect for ages 7+. Height 39".

Lightning Rod

The world's fastest wooden coaster. Launches you 0–73 mph in seconds. For older kids and adults. Height 48".

FireChaser Express

Family launch coaster that goes both forward and backward. Crowd favorite. Height 39".

Smoky Mountain River Rampage

Whitewater rapids ride. You WILL get soaked. Bring ponchos or accept your fate. Height 39".

Mystery Mine

Indoor/outdoor coaster with a 95° drop and storyline scenery. Memorable. Height 48".

Daredevil Falls

Splash-down log flume with a serious drop. Cools you off in summer. Height 42".

Dollywood Express

A real coal-fired steam train tour around the park. Easy break for tired feet. All ages.

Whistle Punk Chaser

Mini family coaster in Wildwood Grove. Perfect first coaster for little ones. Height 36".
