Keep It Classy

Upscale Things to Do in Gatlinburg & the Smoky Mountains

Gatlinburg is known for pancake houses, go-karts, and souvenir t-shirts. But here's a secret: there's a refined, elegant side to the Smokies that most tourists never discover. 🥂

If you're looking to keep it classy on your mountain getaway, this guide is for you. From fine dining to spa experiences, wine tastings to scenic overlooks without the crowds - here's how to experience the sophisticated side of Gatlinburg.

🍷 Fine Dining & Craft Cocktails

The Peddler Steakhouse

A Gatlinburg institution since 1976. Located right on the river, The Peddler serves premium hand-cut steaks with a salad bar that's an experience in itself. Request a riverside table for the best ambiance. Perfect for anniversaries and special occasions.

Cherokee Grill

Upscale American cuisine with gorgeous mountain-lodge décor. Known for their prime steaks, fresh seafood, and craft cocktails. The atmosphere feels elevated without being stuffy.

Crystelle Creek Restaurant

Farm-to-table Southern cuisine with a modern twist. Their trout is locally sourced and their wine list is thoughtfully curated. Intimate setting perfect for a romantic dinner.

The Old Mill Restaurant (Pigeon Forge)

Southern fine dining at its best. The corn chowder is legendary, and the historic mill setting adds charm. Come for lunch to avoid the dinner crowds.

🍇 Wine Tastings & Distillery Tours

Sugarlands Distilling Company

Award-winning craft moonshine and whiskey. Take a distillery tour to learn the history and craft, then enjoy a tasting flight of their premium spirits. Their Roaming Man Tennessee Whiskey is exceptional.

Tennessee Homemade Wines

Sample local wines in a relaxed tasting room. From sweet muscadine to dry reds, they have something for every palate. Grab a bottle to enjoy on the cabin deck later.

Old Forge Distillery

Small-batch bourbon and rum in Pigeon Forge. The intimate tours give you a real behind-the-scenes look at craft distilling. Their coffee-infused rum is a must-try.

💆 Spa & Wellness Experiences

The Spa at Westgate

Full-service luxury spa offering massages, facials, body treatments, and couples packages. Book the hot stone massage after a day of hiking - you'll thank us later.

Spa Lenia at Margaritaville

Island-inspired treatments with mountain views. Their relaxation lounge alone is worth the visit. Try the Paradise Massage or the Volcanic Stone treatment.

In-Cabin Spa Night

Why leave the cabin? At Classy Bear Cabin, your private spa awaits. Sink into the hydrotherapy hot tub under the stars, let the jets melt away the day, and enjoy the mountain views without an appointment or a drive home.

🎭 Arts, Culture & Entertainment

Great Smoky Arts & Crafts Community

Skip the downtown souvenir shops and explore this 8-mile loop of artist studios and galleries. Watch master craftspeople create pottery, leather goods, fine art, and handmade jewelry. These are one-of-a-kind pieces, not mass-produced trinkets.

Dollywood's Premium Shows

Beyond the rides, Dollywood offers Broadway-caliber musical productions. The seasonal shows feature professional performers in state-of-the-art theaters. The Heartsong Theater is particularly impressive.

Sweet Fanny Adams Theatre

Gatlinburg's original comedy theater since 1977. Vaudeville-style shows with clever humor, live music, and genuine entertainment. A fun, sophisticated night out.

🏔️ Scenic Experiences Without the Crowds

Sunrise at Clingmans Dome

The highest point in the Smokies is a tourist hotspot by noon - but arrive at dawn and you'll have it nearly to yourself. The sunrise views are transcendent. Bring coffee in a thermos.

Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail

A one-way loop road through old-growth forest with waterfalls and historic cabins. Take it slow, pull off at the overlooks, and enjoy the peaceful beauty. Early morning is magical.

Private Helicopter Tour

See the Smokies from above with Scenic Helicopter Tours. It's an unforgettable experience and surprisingly affordable. The sunset flights are especially stunning.

Anakeesta at Sunset

Take the chairlift up for dinner at Cliff Top restaurant, then watch the sunset over the mountains with a craft cocktail in hand. Far more refined than the busy downtown below.

🛍️ Upscale Shopping

The Village Shops

A charming European-style shopping village in downtown Gatlinburg. Boutique stores, artisan goods, specialty foods, and unique finds. Far more curated than the typical tourist shops.

Arts & Crafts Community Studios

Commission a custom piece directly from a local artist. Hand-thrown pottery, handcrafted furniture, original paintings - these are investment pieces you'll treasure forever.
