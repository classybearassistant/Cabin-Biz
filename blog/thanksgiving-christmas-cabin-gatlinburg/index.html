Thanksgiving and Christmas Week at a Gatlinburg Cabin

Published November 1, 2026 • 5 min read

Gatlinburg in late November and December is the town at its best: the Parkway lit end to end, Ober Mountain making snow whether the sky cooperates or not, and the Smokies finally visible through bare trees. If you are thinking about a cabin for Thanksgiving or Christmas week, here is what to expect and how to lock it in.

What's open over Thanksgiving

Nearly everything. Dollywood runs its Smoky Mountain Christmas festival from early November through early January, so Thanksgiving weekend has the lights, the shows and the rides. Ober Mountain opens its tubing and skiing as soon as temperatures allow snowmaking, usually by late November, and the aerial tramway and coaster run regardless. Downtown restaurants are busy on Thanksgiving Day itself, so book a table or plan to cook: the cabin's renovated kitchen handles a full turkey dinner, and the dining table seats the whole group.

Winter Magic, Gatlinburg's winter lights display along the Parkway and side streets, is switched on in early November and runs through February. Driving it after dark is a free evening out.

Christmas week at the cabin

Christmas week is the quietest the mountains get and the busiest the town gets. The cabin side is simple: electric fireplaces in every suite, the hot tub on the screened patio with snow sometimes on the railings, and a summit view that is at its clearest once the leaves are down. Most Christmas-week guests spend one day in town for the lights and the rest of the week at the cabin.

Bring a 4WD or AWD vehicle if the forecast shows snow or ice. The cabin is at summit elevation in Chalet Village and the roads up are steep. They are maintained, but a front-wheel-drive sedan on an icy morning is a bad start to the day.

What a 3-bedroom cabin costs for the holidays

Holiday weeks are priced above the rest of the year everywhere in Gatlinburg. At Classy Bear Cabin, Thanksgiving week and the week between Christmas and New Year's typically run from about $450 to $550 a night direct, plus a $225 cleaning fee, with a 2-night minimum. Booking direct is 15% less than the same dates on Airbnb or VRBO, because there is no platform service fee on top.

Three king suites and a loft bunk room mean a family of nine does not need two cabins. Compare that with two hotel rooms downtown at holiday rates and the cabin usually comes out ahead before you count the kitchen.

When to book

Thanksgiving week usually fills by early November and Christmas week by Thanksgiving. The calendar on this site shows live availability; if the dates you want are open, they can be booked in about two minutes. If they are gone, the week after New Year's is the best-value stretch of the whole winter and the snow at the summit is often better.

Questions guests ask

Is the cabin available for Thanksgiving?

Check the live calendar on the booking page. Thanksgiving week typically fills by early November; the week after New Year's is the best-value winter alternative.

Is Dollywood open at Christmas?

Dollywood's Smoky Mountain Christmas festival runs from early November through early January, including Thanksgiving weekend and Christmas week. Confirm specific dates on Dollywood's site before you book tickets.

Do I need 4WD at Christmas?

If snow or ice is forecast, yes. The cabin is at summit elevation on steep, winding roads. In clear weather a standard car is fine.
