The Hot Tub View: What You Actually See from the Patio

Published November 1, 2026 • 3 min read

"Mountain view hot tub" is on half the cabin listings in Gatlinburg. Here is exactly what this one looks at, when it is best, and when it is not.

What you see

The hot tub is on the screened patio on the lower level, facing east over the ridge toward Ober Mountain and the Great Smoky Mountains beyond it. There is no neighboring roof in the sightline; the cabin sits near the top of Chalet Village and the lot drops away below the patio. On a clear morning you see three ridgelines, the Ober tram climbing the near one, and fog sitting in the valley between.

The screening keeps insects out and does not block the view. The patio also holds seating, so people who are not in the tub are not standing around waiting.

By season

Mid-October to early November is the showpiece: the near ridge turns red and orange and the far ridges stay blue. From December through March the leaves are down and the view is widest, with snow at the summit several times most winters. Spring and summer the canopy fills in and the view narrows to the gap between the trees directly in front of the tub: still a mountain view, but a framed one rather than a panorama.

Sunrise is the best time of day year-round. The patio faces the light, and the fog burns off while you watch. Sunset colors the sky behind you rather than in front of you.

The honest caveats

Two of them. In high summer the trees reduce the panorama, as above. And this is a wooded summit lot, so in a heavy rain or fog you will see the trees in front of you and nothing else. On those days the whirlpool tubs inside, with their chromotherapy lighting, do the job instead.

Questions guests ask

Is the hot tub private?

Yes. It is on the cabin's own screened patio, used only by guests of the cabin.

Is the hot tub open in winter?

Yes, year-round. Winter is when the view is widest.

Can you see Ober Mountain from the hot tub?

Yes. The patio faces Ober Mountain; the aerial tram and, in winter, the lit ski slopes are visible from the tub.
