A Cabin for 9 Without Anyone on a Sofa

Published November 1, 2026 • 4 min read

"Sleeps 9" on a listing often means a pull-out sofa and an air mattress. At Classy Bear Cabin it means three king suites, each with its own bathroom, plus a loft with a full-size bunk and a trundle. Nine people, nine real beds, and nobody negotiating over who gets the couch.

How the bedrooms are laid out

Each of the three king suites has a king bed, a private bathroom, an electric fireplace and a smart TV. Two of the suites open directly onto the screened patio with the hot tub. The third sits by the loft, which holds a full-size bunk bed with a trundle underneath: three kids, or two adults and a child, under A-frame windows that get the first light of the morning.

That layout suits three couples as well as it suits a family: every couple gets a door that closes and a bathroom they do not share. Two of the bathrooms were rebuilt in 2026 with rainfall showers and whirlpool tubs with LED chromotherapy lighting.

Where everyone actually spends the day

The main level is open-plan: a living room with floor-to-ceiling windows on the mountain view, a Restoration Hardware dining table that seats the whole group, and a kitchen renovated in 2026 with stainless appliances, quartz counters, drip and Keurig coffee, and full cookware. Downstairs there is a barrel arcade with 60-plus games, a Wii U and board games, which is where the kids disappear to after dinner.

Outside: a private hot tub on the screened patio, a balcony over the mountains, and a BBQ grill. Memorial Day to Labor Day, guests also get a pass to the Chalet Village Owners Club pools and fitness center, under a mile away.

Cabin versus two hotel rooms

For nine people a downtown hotel means at least two rooms, often three, with no kitchen and no shared space except the lobby. The cabin's nightly rate runs from about $200 on a winter weeknight to around $500 for a holiday week, plus a $225 cleaning fee, and the kitchen pays for itself in two dinners. Booking direct is 15% less than Airbnb or VRBO for the same dates.

Questions guests ask

How many beds are there?

Three king beds and a full-size bunk with a trundle: nine sleeping spots in real beds, no sofa beds.

How many bathrooms?

Three full bathrooms, one per king suite, plus a half bath. Two were fully renovated in 2026.

Is there a crib or high chair?

Yes: a Pack 'n Play, a folding high chair, baby gates and children's dinnerware are kept at the cabin.
