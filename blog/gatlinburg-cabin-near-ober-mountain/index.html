Staying 5 Minutes from Ober Mountain

Published November 1, 2026 • 4 min read

From the deck at Classy Bear Cabin you can watch the Ober Mountain tram climb the ridge and, in season, the zip lines and the coaster running below it. The drive to the base is about five minutes. For a ski weekend or a family trip built around the mountain, that is the whole reason to stay in Chalet Village.

What's at Ober Mountain

Ober Mountain (formerly Ober Gatlinburg) is Tennessee's only ski area. Winter brings skiing and snowboarding on ten trails, a snow tubing park, and an indoor ice rink that runs year-round. The rest of the year the draw is the alpine coaster, the mountain coaster and scenic chairlift, zip lines, a wildlife encounter with black bears and otters, and the aerial tramway that runs from downtown Gatlinburg to the summit.

The tram leaves from the Parkway downtown, so you can park once in town, ride up, and see the cabin's ridge from the other side.

Why five minutes matters

Ski days start early and end wet. Being able to drive back for lunch, dry out the kids' gloves, and go back for the afternoon session is the difference between a good ski trip and a long one. On weekends the Ober parking lot fills by mid-morning; leaving from Chalet Village at 8:30 gets you a spot.

In summer the same closeness means an hour on the coaster after dinner without planning a whole evening around it.

Snowmaking and the view

Ober makes its own snow, so the slopes open in late November or early December most years even when the valley is bare. From the cabin's deck you can see whether the lights are on and the snow guns running, which is a better forecast than any app. Once the leaves are down, the summit view runs from Ober across the ridges of the national park.

Questions guests ask

How far is the cabin from Ober Mountain?

About 5 minutes by car. The base area and the ski slopes are visible from the deck.

Can you ski at Ober Mountain at Thanksgiving?

Usually. Ober makes snow once temperatures drop and typically opens skiing and tubing in late November or early December; check Ober's site for the current season.

Does the cabin have ski storage?

There is covered space on the screened patio for boots and boards, and two parking spaces at the door.
