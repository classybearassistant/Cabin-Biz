🥾 Explore the Great Smoky Mountains National Park

There is absolutely NO better way to kick off your Gatlinburg summer getaway than heading straight into the heart of the Great Smoky Mountains National Park — the most visited national park in the entire United States! And the best part? It's completely FREE to enter! Summer transforms the park into a lush, green wonderland, with waterfalls roaring, wildflowers blooming, and wildlife roaming freely. It is truly breathtaking, and every single trail offers something new and incredible to discover!

When it comes to things to do near Gatlinburg in the summer, hiking is at the absolute top of the list, and for great reason. The park boasts over 800 miles of maintained trails, ranging from easy family strolls to heart-pumping summit hikes. Here are some fan favorites you absolutely cannot miss:

  • Alum Cave Trail — A stunning 4.4-mile round trip trail leading to dramatic bluffs and incredible views. Perfect for intermediate hikers!
  • Laurel Falls Trail — One of the most popular paved trails in the park, leading to the gorgeous Laurel Falls waterfall. Great for families!
  • Chimney Tops Trail — A challenging but incredibly rewarding hike with panoramic mountain views at the summit.
  • Clingmans Dome — Drive up and walk a half-mile to the highest point in the Smokies for 360-degree views that will absolutely blow your mind!
  • Rainbow Falls Trail — A 5.4-mile round trip hike to one of the tallest waterfalls in the park — especially stunning on sunny summer afternoons!

Beyond hiking, summer in the Smokies means excellent wildlife watching! Keep your eyes open for black bears (from a safe distance, of course!), white-tailed deer, wild turkeys, and the iconic synchronous fireflies that put on a jaw-dropping light show in late June. It's one of the most magical natural events on Earth, and Gatlinburg is one of the best places in the world to witness it! You'll also want to explore the historic Cades Cove loop road for scenic drives and wildlife sightings, or cool off with some splashing around at the Chimney Picnic Area. Summer in the Smokies is pure, electric adventure — and it's all waiting just minutes from Classy Bear Cabin!

🎢 Thrilling Gatlinburg Attractions & Pigeon Forge Fun

Oh, the excitement doesn't stop at the national park! The Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge corridor is absolutely PACKED with world-class attractions, entertainment venues, and adrenaline-pumping experiences that will keep every member of your group grinning from ear to ear all summer long! Seriously, there is so much to see and do that you might just need to extend your stay — and lucky for you, Classy Bear Cabin makes it incredibly easy to do exactly that!

Here's a thrilling roundup of must-visit attractions that rank among the very best things to do near Gatlinburg this summer:

  • SkyLift Park — Experience the iconic SkyBridge, the longest pedestrian suspension bridge in North America, perched 500 feet above the valley. The views are absolutely breathtaking — and yes, it's a little scary in the best possible way! 😄
  • Anakeesta — This mountaintop village is a summer paradise! Take the chairlift or gondola up, explore treetop skywalk bridges, ride the thrilling rail runner alpine coaster, and enjoy live entertainment against the most stunning backdrop imaginable.
  • Ober Mountain (Ober Gatlinburg) — Don't let the name fool you — summer transforms this ski resort into a full-on adventure park with an alpine slide, wildlife encounter, aerial tram rides, and even indoor ice skating to cool off!
  • Dollywood — Just a short drive in Pigeon Forge, Dollywood is one of the most beloved theme parks in the entire country! World-class roller coasters, live performances, incredible food, and Dolly Parton's legendary charm make this an absolute must-do for every Gatlinburg visitor.
  • Dollywood's Splash Country — Speaking of Dollywood, their water park next door is THE summer destination for beating the heat! Massive water slides, a lazy river, wave pools, and endless splashing fun await!
  • Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies — Right in the heart of Gatlinburg, this stunning aquarium features a jaw-dropping shark tunnel, stingray touch pools, and thousands of incredible sea creatures. An absolute hit with kids and adults alike!
  • WonderWorks — Part science museum, part amusement park, WonderWorks is endlessly entertaining for the whole family with upside-down architecture and over 100 interactive exhibits.
  • Rowdy Bear Mountain Coasters — Feel the wind in your hair as you race down mountain coaster tracks with breathtaking Smoky Mountain views. It's exhilarating, beautiful, and absolutely unforgettable!

And when the sun goes down, the fun keeps going! Stroll the charming streets of downtown Gatlinburg — browse unique shops, indulge in world-famous saltwater taffy, grab a moonshine tasting at one of the many local distilleries, or enjoy a delicious dinner at a cozy mountain restaurant. The Gatlinburg Strip is alive with energy all summer long, and every single evening brings something new and exciting to experience. This is why so many families and couples return year after year — there is genuinely ALWAYS something amazing to do!

🌊 Outdoor Adventures, Hidden Gems & Local Experiences

Beyond the big-name attractions, the Gatlinburg area is absolutely loaded with incredible outdoor adventures and authentic local experiences that will make your summer vacation feel truly one-of-a-kind! If you love getting off the beaten path and discovering hidden treasures, you are going to absolutely LOVE what this region has to offer. These are the kinds of experiences that remind you just how spectacular the natural world really is — and they're all right at your doorstep when you stay at Classy Bear Cabin!

Here are some outstanding adventures and local experiences to add to your summer itinerary:

  • Whitewater Rafting on the Pigeon River — Strap in for an exhilarating ride down the Pigeon River! The Upper Pigeon offers exciting Class III and IV rapids perfect for thrill-seekers, while the Lower Pigeon provides a more relaxed float for families. Multiple outfitters in Hartford, TN offer guided trips — it's one of the most thrilling things to do near Gatlinburg all summer!
  • Zip-lining Over the Smokies — Soar through the forest canopy and experience the mountains from a whole new perspective! Several top-rated zip-line operators in the Gatlinburg area offer multi-line courses with stunning views and heart-pounding thrills.
  • Tubing in Gatlinburg — The Gatlinburg SkyPark and local outfitters offer tubing adventures on area streams — a fantastic way to cool off on a hot summer afternoon while surrounded by gorgeous mountain scenery!
  • Horseback Riding Through the Mountains — Explore the scenic trails of the Smokies on horseback! Several stables near Gatlinburg offer guided rides through meadows, streams, and forested mountain trails. Incredibly peaceful and absolutely beautiful.
  • Visit Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail — This self-guided auto tour takes you through some of the most stunningly beautiful old-growth forest in the Smokies, past rushing streams and historic homesteads. A wonderful and easy summer experience for the whole family!
  • Discover the Gatlinburg Arts Scene — Gatlinburg has a surprisingly vibrant arts community! Visit the Gatlinburg Arts & Crafts Community — the largest community of independent artisans in the US — and discover incredible handcrafted pottery, jewelry, paintings, and more from over 100 local artists along an 8-mile loop.
  • Moonshine & Craft Beer Tasting — Tennessee moonshine is legendary, and Gatlinburg is ground zero for the craft spirit scene! Visit Ole Smoky Distillery, Sugarlands Distilling Company, or Tennessee Shine Co. for free tastings of unique flavors you won't find anywhere else. Truly a one-of-a-kind local experience!
  • Sunset Picnic at Clingmans Dome or Newfound Gap — Pack a gourmet picnic basket with local goodies and head up to the high country for an absolutely magical Smoky Mountain sunset. The colors paint the sky in the most spectacular shades of gold, pink, and purple — it's one of those moments you'll remember forever!

Whether you're seeking heart-pounding adventure or peaceful, soul-restoring moments in nature, the Gatlinburg area in summer delivers it ALL with incredible enthusiasm.
